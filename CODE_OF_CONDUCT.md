# Code of conduct

MotionDeck is maintained by WAYYSTAR Technologies. Discussion around the project should stay usable for people who are here to improve the editor.

## Expected

- Critique the change, not the person.
- Assume a bug report is made in good faith.
- Keep review comments specific enough that the author can act on them.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks
- Publishing someone else’s private information
- Repeatedly filing the same demand after it has been declined

Maintainers may hide or lock threads that break these rules. This is a short project policy, not a legal code.
